# Yunlara — China Furniture Manufacturer-Direct Supplier & Sourcing Partner

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-yunlara.com-0c7c4a)](https://www.yunlara.com)

**Yunlara** is a China-based custom furniture **manufacturer-direct supplier and sourcing partner**. We combine our own design and QC teams with partner factories in Foshan and Dongguan — China's furniture capitals — to deliver factory-level prices and one-stop service to global B2B buyers.

## What We Do

- 🏭 **Manufacturer-direct**: in-house design & QC teams, direct factory pricing
- 🤝 **Sourcing partner**: product sourcing, supplier matching, quality inspection
- 📦 **One-stop service**: design → sampling → production → inspection → export delivery (FOB / CIF / DDP)
- ✏️ **OEM / ODM**: size, material, finish, hardware and branding customization
